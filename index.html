What is Snapshift Puzzles?

Snapshift Puzzles is a free browser-based picture tile puzzle site built for quick, satisfying play. Browse themed categories, take on a fresh Daily Challenge, track medals and streaks, and return later with saved progress on your account. The goal is simple: make every visit feel easy to start, rewarding to finish, and worth coming back for.

Why players keep coming back
A few quick answers before you jump in.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can start playing right away. An account helps with saved progress, daily tracking, medals, and a more personalized Snapshift history.

What makes Snapshift different?

Snapshift combines fast visual puzzle play with a daily system, archive browsing, and progression features so the site feels more like a game destination than a one-off puzzle page.

How often is there something new?

There is a new Daily Challenge every day, plus category browsing for the full library and archive access for players who want to revisit past featured puzzles.

Where should I go next?

Start with the Daily Challenge if you want a single featured puzzle, or browse categories if you want to pick a theme and explore at your own pace.
