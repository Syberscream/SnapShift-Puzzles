Scoring Basics
The four pieces most players care about are Score, Points, Streak, and Swap Cost.

What each part means

  • Score is your main run result and reflects how well you completed the puzzle.
  • Points are the progression value tied to puzzle play and purchases like Swap Hint use.
  • Streak tracks repeated daily completions and makes returning tomorrow matter.
  • Swap Cost is the price of using a Swap Hint, so it trades points for momentum.
Good runs usually come from balancing speed and efficiency rather than forcing a perfect board with too many costly actions.

Best mindset

If you are chasing a better score, solve naturally first and use hints only when they save more than they cost.

If you are protecting a daily streak, finishing cleanly can matter more than squeezing out every last point.

Practical Tips
A few rules of thumb that work well for most Snapshift runs.

For better scores

  • Use Swap Hint sparingly.
  • Read edges and visual clusters before spending points.
  • Keep your board organized so the final moves are cleaner.

For steadier daily results

  • Finish consistently instead of restarting too often.
  • Use a hint late in the run when one good move can unlock the rest.
  • Come back daily to keep your streak moving.
