What information Snapshift may collect
The site is built around lightweight account and puzzle features, so the data used is mostly operational and gameplay-related.

Account information

If you create an account, Snapshift may collect details such as your email address, username, login session data, verification status, and related account identifiers needed to sign you in and keep your profile working.

This information is used to manage authentication, let you return to your account, and connect site features like points, unlocked puzzles, and saved progress to the right player.

Puzzle and gameplay data

Snapshift may store gameplay-related information such as current puzzle state, puzzle completions, daily challenge history, medals, ratings, streaks, unlocked puzzles, points totals, and similar progress details.

That information helps the site resume unfinished puzzles, show category and archive progress, update leaderboards, and power account-based features across devices when available.

Technical and device information

Like most modern sites, Snapshift may process technical data such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, approximate usage times, error states, referring pages, and general performance information.

This kind of information is used to keep the site working well across desktop, tablet, mobile, and foldable devices and to diagnose layout, compatibility, or gameplay issues.

Messages you send directly

If you use a contact page, support email, or another direct channel, Snapshift may receive the information you include in that message, such as your name, email address, screenshots, or support details.

That information is used only to respond, troubleshoot, or follow up on the request you made.

How information is used
Most data is used to run site features, maintain saved progress, and improve the player experience.

To run the site

Information may be used to authenticate users, load puzzle pages, save progress, sync points and unlocks, show daily challenge status, and keep other core Snapshift features working properly.

To improve reliability

Usage and technical information may be used to monitor broken pages, performance issues, layout problems, and device compatibility so Snapshift can be kept stable and easier to use.

To support progression features

Gameplay-related data may be used to calculate medals, show daily completion history, maintain category progress, restore unfinished puzzles, and display account-based stats or rankings.

To respond to support requests

If you contact Snapshift directly, your message and any related details may be used to answer questions, investigate account issues, or help resolve technical problems.

Cookies, browser storage, analytics, and third parties
These technologies help Snapshift remember sessions, preserve puzzle state, and understand how the site is being used.

Cookies and local storage

Snapshift may use cookies and browser storage, including local storage, to remember sign-in state, save preferences, restore unfinished puzzles, track account-related site state, and support gameplay continuity between visits.

If you clear browser storage or block it entirely, some features such as remembered sessions, saved state, or progress restoration may stop working as expected.

Analytics

Snapshift may use site analytics to understand traffic, page performance, feature usage, and technical behavior. That helps improve layout stability, puzzle flow, and overall site quality.

Analytics information is used for site measurement and improvement rather than for public player-facing gameplay features.

Third-party services

Snapshift may rely on outside service providers for hosting, analytics, email delivery, or other site operations. Those providers may process limited information when needed to perform those services.

If Snapshift enables additional third-party services later, this policy should be updated to describe them more specifically.

Advertising

If Snapshift serves third-party advertising in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and any applicable consent or privacy choices that appear on the site.

Where regional privacy or consent requirements apply, additional notices or choices may be shown to players.

Your choices and policy updates
Players can control some browser-level settings directly and can contact Snapshift for privacy-related questions.

Your browser choices

You can clear cookies, local storage, and saved site data in your browser at any time. Keep in mind that doing so may remove remembered sessions, saved puzzle progress, and some convenience features.

Account and support questions

If you need help with an account, want to report a privacy concern, or need help understanding how a feature stores progress, use the Contact page so Snapshift can respond directly.

Policy changes

Snapshift may update this privacy policy as the site grows, adds features, or changes how services are used. When this page is updated, the date near the top of the page should also be refreshed.
