How to play

Swap tiles, lock them in, beat the clock.

The basics

  • Drag a tile onto another to swap them, or click one tile and then another. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and Enter.
  • A tile that lands in its correct place locks and can’t move again.
  • Pick a difficulty: Easy (4×6), Medium (6×9) or Hard (8×12).
  • Hard mode hides the green “correct” outline and pays ×1.45 points.

Score and streak

Each tile you lock in adds to your score. Locking tiles on consecutive moves builds a streak that makes each one worth more; a swap that fixes two tiles at once earns a bonus. A swap that fixes nothing resets the streak.

Medals

When you finish, your run is rated on move efficiency (72%: the fewest swaps possible is one per tile) and speed (28%: against a par time per tile).

  • ★★★ Gold – 90% or better
  • ★★ Silver – 76% or better
  • ★ Bronze – any finish

Points

Finishing a puzzle pays a base amount for the difficulty (40 / 60 / 80) plus a speed bonus. You get full points the first time you finish a puzzle on a difficulty and a quarter after that. The daily challenge adds a 50-point bonus, and daily streaks pay extra at 7, 14 and 30 days.

Spend points to unlock puzzles (the first in every category is free) and on helpers.

Helpers

  • Swap hint highlights a good swap. Free on normal puzzles, 60 points on the daily, with a 15-second cooldown.
  • Place 1 tile makes the best swap for you (30 / 45 / 60 points by difficulty). Not available on the daily.

Daily challenge

Always Hard (8×12), and everyone gets the same shuffle. Your first finish of the day counts for the leaderboard and your streak; you can replay as often as you like.
