Delete your SnapShift Puzzles account

This page explains how to delete your account in SnapShift Puzzles (the website snapshiftpuzzles.com and the SnapShift Puzzles app on Google Play, by Syberscream) and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in at snapshiftpuzzles.com/account (or in the app: Menu → Account → Sign in).
  2. Come back to this page, or open Account and choose Delete my account at the bottom.
  3. Enter your password and press Delete my account permanently.

Your account is deleted straight away. If you can’t sign in, use the contact page with the email address on your account and we’ll delete it for you within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, username and password.
  • Your games and progress: saved games, points, unlocks, medals, achievements, streaks, daily, Time Attack, Slider and event results, challenges and picture requests.
  • Your public profile and your entries on the leaderboards.
  • Daily reminder notifications linked to your account, messages you sent through the contact form, and invite records.

What is kept

  • If you had SnapShift Plus, the payment records that Stripe (our payment provider) must keep by law stay with Stripe. Any active subscription is stopped so you’re not charged again.
  • Short-lived server logs used for security are deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Nothing else is kept. Deleting an account can’t be undone.

Games played as a guest are only stored on your own device. To remove them, clear this site’s data in your browser, or uninstall the app.
