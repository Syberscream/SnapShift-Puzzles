Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, username and a securely hashed password.
  • Gameplay: your games and moves, finishes, medals, points, unlocks, daily results and streaks.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form.
  • Notifications (only if you turn them on): your browser’s push address for this site, your timezone and the hour you picked, so we can send one daily puzzle reminder.
  • Invites: if you joined through a friend’s invite link, which player invited you.
  • Technical: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser) used for security and fixing problems.

How we use it

To run the game: sign you in, save progress, award points, show leaderboards (your username only), send account emails such as verification and password resets, and answer your messages. We don’t sell your information.

Cookies and storage

We use one sign-in cookie. Your browser’s local storage keeps guest games, sound and difficulty preferences on your device.

Service providers

The site runs on Cloudflare. Account emails are sent through an email delivery provider. SnapShift Plus payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details. We keep your Stripe customer id and membership dates so we know your membership is active.

Android app

The SnapShift Puzzles app on Google Play opens this website full screen, so the same information and choices on this page apply to it. The app itself collects nothing extra.

Your choices

You can turn daily reminder notifications off on your account page or in your browser or phone settings at any time. You can change your password at any time. To delete your account and data, see Delete your account. For any other privacy question, use the contact page.
