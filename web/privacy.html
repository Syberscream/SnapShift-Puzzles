Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, username and a securely hashed password.
  • Gameplay: your games and moves, finishes, medals, points, unlocks, daily results and streaks.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form.
  • Technical: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser) used for security and fixing problems.

How we use it

To run the game: sign you in, save progress, award points, show leaderboards (your username only), send account emails such as verification and password resets, and answer your messages. We don’t sell your information.

Cookies and storage

We use one sign-in cookie. Your browser’s local storage keeps guest games, sound and difficulty preferences on your device.

Service providers

The site runs on Cloudflare. Account emails are sent through an email delivery provider. Supporter payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details.

Your choices

You can change your password at any time. To delete your account and data, or for any privacy question, use the contact page.
