About SnapShift Puzzles

Quick daily play and satisfying picture puzzle sessions.

SnapShift is a browser picture-tile puzzle game. Every puzzle is a picture cut into a grid of tiles and shuffled; swap tiles until the picture is whole again. Tiles lock in place when they land in the right spot, so every good move feels like progress.

Ways to play

  • Daily Challenge – one featured puzzle a day, the same board for everyone, with a leaderboard and streak rewards.
  • Categories – a growing library of themed collections: animals, landscapes, space, food, cities, fractals and more.
  • Daily Archive – replay any past daily.

Progress

With a free account your games save automatically and follow you between devices. Finish puzzles to earn points and medals, unlock more puzzles and build a daily streak.

New puzzles

New pictures are added regularly. Some are created with AI image tools and every one is reviewed by a person before it goes live.
