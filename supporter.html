Frequently Asked Questions

Why become a Supporter?
Your support helps fund new puzzles, Daily Challenge improvements, site upgrades, and future premium features while giving you a cleaner browsing experience.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly Supporter plans are intended to be cancel-anytime plans. Annual plans are best for regular players who want the lower yearly rate.
Will free play still be available?
Yes. Snapshift Puzzles will continue to offer free play, Daily Challenges, medals, streaks, and saved progress.
